# Chesterton fences (backend)

Do not remove without understanding why it exists.

1. **gRPC `max_retries <= 0` → 3** — proto3 cannot distinguish omit vs zero; mapping uses `QUEUE_DEFAULT_*` when `<=0` (since `0.1.107`; instant-DLQ fix landed in `0.1.106`). Changing back to “0 means zero retries” breaks omit clients.
2. **JWT re-checked against DB** — claims alone are insufficient after key revoke/queue-scope change.
3. **Stripe event dedupe + monotonic timestamp** — prevents stale resends re-granting tiers after cancel.
4. **Idempotency unique index filtered WHERE key IS NOT NULL** — allows many jobs without keys without colliding on NULL.
5. **Completion webhook optional HMAC** — default unsigned (existing receivers); `completion_webhook_secret` opts into org-webhook-compatible signing without forcing all consumers.
6. **RLS present but unused** — honesty path chosen over fake security theater; enabling RLS needs GUC-per-query and role changes — large migration.
7. **Rate-limit fail-open by default** — self-host without Redis keeps working; set `RATE_LIMIT_FAIL_CLOSED=true` on hosted SaaS.
8. **Admin constant-time compare after hash** — do not “simplify” to string `==`.
9. **Unverified billing email is ignored by email login** (0.1.117) — do not "simplify" the login lookup back to `billing_email = $1`; that reopens identity squatting (BE-42).
10. **Fall-back repeat guard skipped only for interval schedules** (0.1.117) — minute `*`/`*/N` or hour `*`; a step hour with a fixed minute keeps the guard (else `0 */2` double-fires). The guard must jump past the reference wall clock rather than to the next day (BE-45).
11. **`parent_job_id` is SET NULL, plus the `"A_cancel_blocked_dependents_on_delete"` trigger** (0.1.117) — switching back to CASCADE silently deletes live children; dropping or renaming the trigger (it must sort before `RI_ConstraintTrigger_*`) lets dependents of never-completed jobs run after the orphan sweep.
12. **Legacy `lookup_hash IS NULL` keys are still scanned** (0.1.119, operator decision) — the scan is bounded (bcrypt gate; one scan at a time per transport, REST/login and gRPC on separate turns; a wait that covers one scan at the measured verify speed, capped at 10 s, then 503; concurrent requests for one token share one scan; negative cache; per-IP budget on REST and one shared budget on gRPC, both charged before the scan and refunded when the key is found) rather than removed; do not rotate, deactivate or drop legacy keys, do not gate the record-cache or `lookup_hash` fast path on the budget, do not merge the two scan turns back into one (a gRPC flood then locks REST legacy keys out, W3R-08), and do not answer a scan that never ran with 401 (SDKs do not retry it, W3R-07) (BEAUTH-05, BEGRPC-13).
13. **Oversized jobs are dead-lettered on gRPC claim** (0.1.119) — a job over 4 MiB cannot be sent, and skipping it would block the queue head; do not turn that into a silent skip or an unbounded retry (BEGRPC-04).
14. **gRPC `max_concurrency` is clamped, not rejected** (0.1.119) — proto3 cannot say "unset" and fleets that sent up to 1000 must keep registering; same for the empty hostname and the 255-byte claim-path queue-name limit (BEGRPC-09).
15. **Draining workers count toward the cap only while they heartbeat** (0.1.119) — counting them for ever would let a crashed draining worker hold a slot; not counting them was the bypass (BEGRPC-15).
16. **An omitted gRPC lease is 300 s, not the REST 30 s** (0.1.119, operator decision) — `GRPC_DEFAULT_LEASE_DURATION_SECS` exists because the Go SDK `StreamJobs` cannot send a lease and Go/PHP `Dequeue`/`RenewLease` send 0 unless set; "unifying" it with `WORKER_LEASE_DURATION_SECS` re-delivers their running jobs to a second worker (W3R-03). For the same reason `0` means unset (300), never the 5 s minimum, and a value at or below `WORKER_HEARTBEAT_INTERVAL_SECS` refuses to start.
17. **A legacy Payment Link checkout never cancels and needs a verified billing email** (0.1.120, BEBILL-02) — `client_reference_id` is a URL parameter anyone can edit; do not "restore" linking by org id alone or let the unverified path retire a subscription (that let anyone take over an org's billing and cancel its real subscription). Only `metadata.spooled_org_id` (set by `POST /billing/checkout`) is trusted.
18. **Replaced subscriptions live in `stripe_superseded_subscription_ids` until Stripe confirms the cancel, and `subscription.updated` for a non-current subscription is ignored while the current one is live** (0.1.120, BEBILL-14) — checkout reuses the customer, so without the guard `subscription.created(new)` hides the old subscription before `checkout.completed` and it is never cancelled; without the list a failed cancel is unretryable and the customer is billed twice. Never cancel the org's current subscription.
19. **Edge client-IP headers count only from an internal TCP peer** (0.1.120, BEAUTH-10) and IPv6 ids are /64s (BEBILL-24) — reading `CF-Connecting-IP` / `X-Forwarded-For` directly, or keying on a full IPv6 address, lets a caller choose or mint its own rate-limit bucket. Production's cloudflared is a private peer; `TRUSTED_CLIENT_IP_HEADER` is for proxies on public addresses.
20. **The per-code login attempt cap (40) stays above the per-address ceiling (20)** (0.1.120, BEAUTH-06) — lowering it back to 5 re-creates "a stranger locks the owner out". Turnstile fails closed and the code row is written only after the mail is sent (a provider outage must not burn the 5 codes per hour; W4-03 is the cost).
21. **Soft delete is one transaction and one-way** (0.1.120, BEFLOW-06) — keys, schedules, outgoing webhooks, webhook token and `plan_tier = 'deleted'` change together; the cron query and the custom-webhook ingest filter on `plan_tier <> 'deleted'`. Restoring `plan_tier` is not an undelete.
22. **`custom_retention_days()` uses a nested `CASE` on purpose** (migration `20261012110100`) — Postgres does not promise `AND` evaluation order, so flattening it can let the INT cast see a value the guard rejected and fail the retention sweep for every org.
23. **Retention keeps a workflow's failed and cancelled steps until the workflow row is gone, and locks what it deletes** (0.1.121, W5-R4/W5-R5) — dropping the `NOT EXISTS (workflows)` guard lets a workflow retry run a dead-lettered step's dependents ungated (the step's dependency edges cascade away with it); dropping `FOR UPDATE ... SKIP LOCKED` deletes a job a concurrent retry just revived. Every FK column that references `jobs(id)` needs an index, or each retention batch scans that table once per deleted job (W5-R6).
24. **`idx_jobs_claim` and the claim queries change together, and a concurrent-index migration is one statement** (0.1.121, BEJOB-03) — the index is partial on `status IN ('pending', 'scheduled')` with the claim's sort order; a query that widens the status list, drops the organization/queue equalities or reorders stops using it and sorts the whole backlog again. The migration has no `SET lock_timeout` and no second statement because sqlx sends a `-- no-transaction` file as one query string and Postgres refuses `CREATE INDEX CONCURRENTLY` inside the implicit transaction block of a multi-statement string; the `...100001` / `...140001` / `...140101` repairs exist because an interrupted build leaves an `INVALID` index that `IF NOT EXISTS` skips for ever.
25. **`queue_in_key_scope()` is inlinable SQL over an `IMMUTABLE` helper** (0.1.121, migration `20261013130200`) — a `STABLE` call or a sub-query in it keeps the prefix-pattern arm in every plan and a scoped key's counts scan the organization's job range (1.5 ms to 43 ms at 130k jobs); the one place where `queue_name = ANY(...)` is right is inside the function, for exact names.
26. **Job and DLQ retry re-open only sweep-cancelled dependents, and the DLQ call skips instead of failing at the workflow cap** (0.1.121, BEFLOW-04, W5-R3) — re-opening any cancelled child would resurrect work someone cancelled; refusing a whole bulk retry because one old step's workflow does not fit blocks every other job. The single-job retry still refuses (429/403) because skipping the slot charge reopens the W4-01 cap bypass. A scoped key's retry reaching dependents outside its scope is a reviewed design note (W5-R1).
27. **A WebSocket `1008` close means "do not reconnect with this token"; `1000` and `1011` mean reconnect** (0.1.121, BEFLOW-12/13) — clients (dashboard `auth_failed`) key on it, so do not use `1008` for a transient failure or `1011` for an expiry.
28. **`q` never compares text with a payload, and the job-type queries repeat the index predicate** (0.1.122, W8-R2) — the first version of `job_type` / `q` walked `idx_jobs_org_created` and read every payload from TOAST (381 / 466 ms at 130k jobs, no timeout). `q` resolves the matching job types by a skip scan of `idx_jobs_org_job_type` (first 1000 distinct types) and probes each through the index; "simplifying" it back to `payload->>'job_type' ILIKE` or dropping `octet_length(...) <= 255` from a query undoes that. The statement timeout around the list is the backstop, not the fix.
29. **The list statement timeout is `SET LOCAL` in a read-only transaction and its error is a 400** (0.1.122, W8-R2) — a session `SET` leaks to the next pooled user; a 5xx makes the SDKs retry a request that would only time out again and feeds their circuit breakers. `0` is refused at boot because Postgres reads it as no timeout.
30. **The worker counter trigger is a deferred constraint trigger, gated by a live-lease `WHEN`** (0.1.122, QW15, W8-R3) — immediate, it deadlocked a worker completing a job and its `any`-mode dependent (225 of 300 rounds); the `OLD.lease_expires_at > now()` test is what keeps sweeps and the too-large gRPC dead-letter (which expires the lease first) out of `jobs_failed`. Do not count in Rust, do not make it immediate, do not drop the `WHEN` (it is also what keeps the per-row cost near zero).
31. **Workflow outcomes are claimed (committed) before they are dispatched** (0.1.122, QW8, W8-R4) — at most once is the design; dispatching first duplicates under several instances, and at-least-once needs a durable outbox. The `id = ANY(ARRAY(...))` shape and the predicate/order shared with `idx_workflows_unannounced_outcome` keep the pass off a seq scan of `workflows`. Retry paths null `completed_at` so the next outcome is announced.
32. **`job.deadlettered` comes from the dispatcher, and `X-RateLimit-Reset` is an epoch** (0.1.122, QW8, QW9, W8-R1) — an explicit `job.deadlettered` emit doubles the event; a delta in `X-RateLimit-Reset` reads as January 1970 in all four SDKs. `Retry-After` is the wait.
33. **`jobs_partitioned` is dormant scaffolding; do not follow the "swap tables" notes of migration `20241209000009`** (wave 9, BEJOB-17, deferred) — the table is empty and unread. Its idempotency index includes `created_at` (PostgreSQL requires the partition key in a unique index), so `ON CONFLICT (organization_id, idempotency_key)` has no unique index to infer and duplicate keys would stop being detected; it also lacks six `jobs` columns, every foreign key to `jobs(id)`, the CHECKs and the triggers. Partitioning the live table needs a purpose-built migration tested on a copy of production; removing the scaffolding needs a migration of its own (an applied migration is never edited: sqlx checksums).
